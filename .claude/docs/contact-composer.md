Phone untouched: MSG routes to #my-lisst. **CONTACT STAGE 2.0 (2026-08-04, one long react session — his six-item list plus three rounds on top; in-file comments at `bdmShape`/`bdmClearTf`/`bdmFold`/`bdmAskBox`/`.bdm-form` are the mechanics truth). Items 1-5 of the six are DONE; item 6 (the mailed envelope tossed back into the deck) is the only one left.** **THE PAPER IS AN ENVELOPE**: `bdmShape()` fits a real #10 rectangle (`ENV_TUNE.ratio` 2.303; DL 2.0 and C6 1.42 written beside it) into the space the stage has — the box used to take whatever the flex line left, so its SHAPE was a side effect of the viewport and went near-square on a tall window. It is JS and not CSS `aspect-ratio` because aspect-ratio stops preserving the ratio the moment a max-height clamps an explicit width, i.e. at exactly the sizes this exists to fix. It writes whole-px W/H, so **the shape IS the lock** — the fold needs no separate one — and it runs when the pull lands so the four dots settle before anything is drawn between them. Also sets `--envk` (the interior's scale, W/610 clamped `ENV_TUNE.kMin`-`kMax` = .88-2.4, TYPE floors at 8/9.5/10px on top of it) and `--envw` on `.bay-dm`. **`maxW` 920 → 1440 and `kMax` 1.35 → 2.4 (2026-08-04, "raise the max").** 920 was set when the paper was much smaller and had never actually bound, so it went unnoticed until the row rebalance made the envelope big enough to hit it — on a 1990×1414 window the HEIGHT path wanted 1446 and the cap held it at 920. The cap's real job is only to stop a wide, SHORT window making a comically long envelope; height is the honest constraint and is already self-limiting. **kMax MUST TRACK maxW and that is why it is a named knob now, not a literal:** `--envk` is W/610, so the old 1.35 ceiling bit at W=824 — past that the paper grew and the writing did NOT (already 12% behind at the old cap; would have been 74% behind at 1440, a big sheet with small type). 2.4 sits just above 1440/610 = 2.361 so the ceiling never bites before maxW does, making the envelope an EXACT photographic enlargement at every size — **the same law the cards already follow** (ONE CARD SPEC). If maxW rises again, raise kMax past maxW/610 in the same edit. kMin .88 is untouched: that one is a legibility floor, not a proportion, and is meant to bite on short stages. **THE PIXEL-GRID REVERSAL — READ THIS BEFORE 'FIXING' SUB-PIXEL GEOMETRY HERE**: the previous pass's `bdmSnapBox` (a sub-pixel translate onto the whole-px grid) was CAUSING the blur it promised to fix — measured at DPR 1, the top silhouette rendered 55/100/100/41 across four device rows with it and 0/0/0 on three whole rows without. Chrome already snaps this subtree's paint; an explicit fractional transform is honoured ON TOP of that. Gone, replaced by `bdmClearTf`, whose comment carries the measurement. What actually delivers crispness: integer box + .5-centred coordinates + ODD stroke widths. **This box must stay transform-free** (`envPress` and the error jostle ride the independent `scale`/`translate` properties for that reason). **ONE SVG OWNS THE ENVELOPE** (`bdmEnvDraw`, role layers: silhouette → fills → creases → seams → apex). The old ghost-line bug was a resize re-running `bdmPlineGeom`, whose innerHTML rebuild wiped the per-path visibility the fold had written; the composer's line svg now goes dark WHOLESALE (`.folding`) and `bdmPlineGeom` bails while an envelope exists — hiding the parent can't be undone by a child rebuild. **THE BACK WALL** is a second svg seated BEFORE the stuffing clones (silhouette + the top crease, the only crease a proud card reaches): the boards are in the pocket, so the back panel passes behind them and the folded flaps in front. `backForward()` hands both nodes to the front stack on the shuv's beat, because the top flap's fill lands exactly on the top crease and a polygon edge on a line eats half of it. **THE BOARDS GO IN SHOVED** (`STUFF_TUNE`): sized off the ENVELOPE (`fillH .80`, his number) not the opening, fanned, crooked, size-jittered, outer cards riding lower, one per 130ms, then HELD half a second — without the hold the last card was still arriving as the flap closed and the beat never existed. At .80 they stand proud of the top edge, so `stuffSink()` has the flap SHOVE them in as it comes over (same x/angle/size, only the seat moves; the sunk pose fit-clamps its scale because a crooked card is taller than its own height). Dylan on seeing it: *"the addition of the 'shuv' at the end looks awesome."* **THE SEAL IS THROWN** (`SEAL_TUNE`): drops at the top of the stage at 1.9x, lands with the card-into-deck squash + kthunk, holds, rides one bezier at the envelope shrinking and turning, stamps OFF-AXIS. **SECOND-PRESS LAW built**: a second SEND mid-ceremony settles it sealed and armed (one `alive` gate stands every rAF and timer in the closure down without touching `bdmN`). **ONE CONTACT QUESTION** (his call — "how we talk and where do we reach you are essentially the same question"): the `CHAN` table gives each channel a send mode — `open` (the platform's compose opens with the letter in it; a tick and a note fill the slot the field would have used, so the row keeps its shape and the absent field is a statement) · `post` (splitforms, and a handle is the ONE required field on the form) · `none`. The mark beside the label swaps to the chosen channel (phosphor brand glyphs are fill-based, hence `brandi`); a native `<select>` cannot put icons in its own list and a custom dropdown would trade keyboard and mobile behaviour for decoration. **The required jostle** (`bdmNope` + `kbonk`, the site's first negative voice — two dry low blips through a lowpass) fires ONLY on the post paths, throws the card TOWARD the offending field and focuses it. **THE ASK DROPDOWN RETIRED** ("we arnt guessing the ask. we know they are interested. they made it this far") — the default letter is the greeting running into the tag column. **THE LETTER IS EDITABLE, THE TAGS ARE NOT**: a board's line body and its ask box are THE SAME STRING (either end edits it, no parsing, no second source of truth); emptying it in the letter resets that board's ask; every other line takes a `bdmOver` override the moment you type in it and its dropdown takes it back the moment you use the dropdown. The tags survive because they live OUTSIDE the editable region — you cannot backspace through something that was never in the text. One line per board, always, in the drawer head's own chip (black fill, white letters). **THE TAGS POINT BOTH WAYS**: hovering one lights its board with the full card treatment via `.lit` — the hover build WITHOUT a cursor, which SHARES the `:hover` rules' own declaration blocks (`:is(.d7f:hover, .d7f.lit)`, the `.hold`/`.redress` law: a copy drifts and a drift blinks) with the entry/exit decode extracted to `cardDressOn`/`cardDressOff`. The drawer head's manifest got the same link. TWO SIGNALS, TWO MEANINGS: hover GROWS the chip, the caret in a board's ask box INVERTS its tag (wiped from the left, held for the whole focus). **THE SPLIT BUTTON** (`.bdm-ask`): one control covering both asks — MAKE AN OFFER | MORE INFO?, press either and the box becomes the input with the caret in it, the chip holds which and is the way out, height reserved in both states, and a chosen half with nothing typed still says something (`ASK_DEF`). **THE SEND** is envelope-anchored: two thirds the paper's width, centred in the gap below it (auto margins on the main axis, and `ENV_TUNE.sendAir` holds room back or the ratio-fit takes every pixel and there is nothing to centre in — the air comes off the dealt ROW via `rowMax`, because the row is the REMINDER and the envelope is the thing being used). Black fill, verb big, DESTINATION under it in the same button — the verb changes with the state, the destination never does. It is the LAST thing that animates: a clip-path chip fill left→right fired off `bdmApply`'s RETURNED tail (the arrival's real duration, since a hardcoded beat finished 200ms before the letter did and would break again at a different deck size). Its destination decodes on first entry and on channel change only — `bdmHref` runs on every keystroke, so a blanket decode would scramble it while you type. **THE COMPOSER IS TWO HALVES** (his annotation): the controls are a TRAY with their own hairline outline (1px against the controls' 1.5px — a boundary, not a fifth box), the message is paper with an indent and no frame, and `.bdm-body`'s inset is the drawn frame's own 12.5px line PLUS real air — it was a flat 16px, three and a half pixels of clearance, which reads as a mistake rather than a margin. **EVERY REF IS THE SAME WIDTH**: `padRef` zero-pads the leading integer at the ref PRODUCERS (HS 6 → HS 06), so cards, the lb header, the manifest and the letter print one string; decimal refs (HS 22.5) pass through. That surfaced a mismatch older than the pass — `refOf` fell through to "#01" on originals while the card's own chip printed "OG 01" — so **`refOf(b, k, c)` now delegates to `jref` when it has the collection**. The demo maps (`JSPARK`/`JPEND`/`JGONE`/`PH_DIMS`) are keyed by the ref string and moved with it. **THE ROW PAYS AND THE PAPER TAKES (2026-08-04, his react on the stage's layout — "take the height off the row and give it to the envelope. the envelope is most important here", plus "the cards need more breathing room horizontally and ... vertically from their ask/offer buttons" with explicit licence to shrink them: "im not precious about how the cards scale up top").** `rowMax` .34 → **.30, and it BITES at every size now on purpose** — the row is sized by what it can spare, not what it would like. The gain is LEVERED: the envelope is ratio-locked and height-bound, so 1px of row height freed becomes **2.303px of envelope WIDTH** (671→781 at 1440×900; row 240→212; cards 119→88). **The dealt row's spacing is ONE air value** — `--dealAir` 24px, which is `ENV_TUNE.airMin`, the same white the send floats in — **corrected for the card's own shadow reach in each direction per the SHADOW LAW** (2.5862% of card width right, 3.4483% down): the old flat gaps were 15px of real white horizontally and **6px** under the card, the `.bdm-body` mistake-not-a-margin again. `bdmDealW` SOLVES the row's height budget against `--dealAir` instead of a hardcoded 60, so the CSS air and the JS cap cannot drift — raise the air and the cards pay for it with no second edit. **The ask buttons bottom-align into a straight band** (`.bdm-row` stretch + `.bdm-ask` margin-top auto): the fv3 card is NOT fixed-aspect — its name row is content-height, so a wrapped name ("PURPLE PURPLE" at the 78px floor) is 8px taller and dragged that one button out of line; consequence, `--dealAir` is the MINIMUM white under a card, which is the right way round since the band is shared furniture and the air is not. **THE ASK LABELS GO SHORT** as the second rung on the existing `tight` ladder (same threshold that drops the icons): OFFER | INFO?, because at 88px cards "Make an offer" broke to three lines in a 40px box. The long copy is kept for stages tall enough to afford it (a 4K drawer computes ~223px cards), so it is a swap, not a rewrite. **THE FLOOR ON `rowMax` IS THE BUTTON, NOT THE CARD** — the split halves need ~40px of text room each, i.e. a ~96px card, so pushing this knob lower means shortening labels again. **"OR THE LONG FORM →" IS GONE** (his call): it was the one item below the send, charging the envelope its own height plus a stage gap for a link the LISST organ already carries — not orphaned, just not advertised from inside the composer, which is the one place someone has already chosen how to write. **TWO PRE-EXISTING BUGS FELL OUT OF VERIFYING IT, both about measuring a moving target:** (1) **`bdmShape` was solving a FIXED POINT with one subtraction** — `--envk` is set from W, the send's padding and type scale off `--envk`, and the send's height is one of the sibling heights W is solved against, so a single pass measured a send built for the PREVIOUS envelope. Now iterated to convergence (reading offsetHeight flushes layout, so each pass sees the send the pass before it caused); the feedback is negative so it converges, and an oscillating pair takes the SMALLER — can be a pixel off, cannot overflow. (2) **the resize refit debounced 80ms past the layout cascade, but `--caseH` is DRIVER-EASED**, so it measured a height still in transit and, because nothing re-ran it, kept that shape for good: 1024×768 → 1440×900 shipped a **711px envelope and 79px cards where a fresh load computes 781 and 88** — silently the small-window layout on a big window.
