## Deck-click during inspection

- **Deck-click during inspection — SHIPPED 2026-08-01 (desktop).** The 2026-07-22 innermost-layer rule is REVERSED for drawer-background clicks: a drawer click is a DRAWER click whether or not a card is being inspected, and the lightbox is never backed out of. The `bayPanel` handler simply lost its `if (!lb.hidden) { closeLb(); return; }` short-circuit, so the existing state machine runs unchanged — Dylan's rules, 2026-08-01: **click in an OPENING (or mid-closing) drawer finishes the open and locks it; click in a fully OPEN drawer closes it; neither touches the lightbox.** The reasoning worth keeping: the drawer is the thing under the cursor, so dismissing a different surface was the click going somewhere the user wasn't pointing. The flagged edge got Dylan's answer — **a drawer-originated inspection goes BACK to the drawer**, so `lbToShelfArc` reopens it before flying (desktop-guarded). An inspection can outlive the drawer it came from now, and the arc would otherwise land a card on a shelf inside a shut case; `getEnd` re-measures every frame, so the card tracks its shelf while the drawer is still sliding out under the flight, exactly as the add flight does. **AND THEN IT SHUTS AGAIN** (Dylan, same day: *"if someone just closed it they probably want it closed"*) — a drawer that was SHUT only comes back to catch the card and drains behind it (`bayShutAfterLanding`, consumed in the arc's landing callback AFTER `landBeat`, so it never closes out from under the card); a drawer that was already open is left alone. The reopen is **`openBay(transient)`, which skips `shopRoom`** — the net drawer state is unchanged, and a room reflow out and straight back inside a second reads as jank. That is only safe because **`shopRoom` guards itself on `body.bay-room` rather than on a transition**, so a REAL open during the flight still takes the room correctly (verified) and the paired `closeBay` is then a free no-op; the same self-guard is why `openBay`/`closeBay` lost their old `was` bookkeeping, which was duplicating it. `openBay` clears the flag, so any real open during the flight wins; the flag is ASSIGNED (not OR'd) per arc so a killed arc cannot leave it armed. The drain re-arms like any explicit close, so it cannot creep back open under a cursor parked near the edge. Mobile is untouched by construction: the takeover case's door handlers are all lb-guarded, so the case cannot be closed while the lb is up and there is nothing to reopen. Verified in real Chrome at 1440 (the pane's rAF is dead — 0 frames in 1.5s — and every bay state change poses through `bayTick`, so the pane cannot test this at all): mid-drain click re-locks with the lb up · fully-open click drains to bare `bay` with the lb up · Esc on a drawer-orphaned case inspection reopens the drawer, lands the card visible and drains behind it with `bay-room` never taken · an already-open drawer stays open with its room · a mid-flight reopen keeps the drawer open AND takes the room. No stray `.bay-fly`, no stranded `.preseat` on any branch.
