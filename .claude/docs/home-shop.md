## What the surface is

Everything that isn't an inspection, a deck or a message: the landing you arrive at, the shop you browse, and the pages a collection opens into. It is ONE document — `#home` and `#colpage` are two siblings and `route()` flips their `hidden` on the hash, so there are no page loads anywhere on this site and every "page" is a render. **THE SHOWROOM LAW (why home is different from every other route):** home is the only page with no masthead name, no colnav, no swipe-open and no relay word — Dylan's rule is that the mark names it, so `barWordFor("")` returns `""` on purpose and the home bands are exempt from the scroll relay. Judge additions here against that: home states the brand and the goods, and hands off; the working surfaces are the collection pages, the lightbox and YOUR PICKS.

## The landing: masthead, the logo that draws itself, the load intro

`#home` is a `.split` — `.spine` (the fixed left column: masthead, tiles, hero video, story) beside `<main class="shop" id="cols">`. **THE MASTHEAD IS AN INLINED SVG NOW (2026-08-16, thread 1 — "THE LOGO DRAWS ITSELF... the home landing wears the single-stroke draw"):** `#logo-draw` inside `.brand#home-link`, viewBox `-1 0 982.142857 1045`, two anchor `<circle>` dots plus THREE paths — the monogram drawn as ONE continuous stroke (a single `<path>` with `pathLength="1"`, its per-segment pacing baked into the `values`/`keyTimes` pairs of a single `<animate>` so the pen speeds and slows the way a hand would) and the circle closed as two arcs beginning together at 1.136s. **It is inlined for TIMELINE CONTROL, and the source artwork stays in the vault** (`avrg-single-stroke-aligned(2).svg`) — the drawing is the point, and an `<img>` cannot be scrubbed. Stroke dress lives in page CSS (`#logo-draw path { fill: none; stroke: currentColor }`), so **colour is `currentColor` and the old PNG's `filter: invert(1)` retired with the PNG** — one mark, both grounds, no second file. **`playLogo()` REWINDS IT, and that is the whole trick:** SMIL `begin`s run on wall-clock from document load, so a boot on a collection page would leave the drawing long finished by the first visit home; `setCurrentTime(0)` at every home arrival (route()'s else branch, and `touchdown()` in the menu-select ceremony) makes the draw an ARRIVAL beat rather than a load beat. Reduced motion jumps to `setCurrentTime(9)`, past the timeline's end, and `fill="freeze"` holds the finished mark — **the logo is content, the drawing is theater.** `#tick` under it is the decode line (`startTick`).

**THE LOAD INTRO (2026-08-16, thread 3, PHONE ONLY — his spec: "on initial site load, the logo draw needs to be the only thing on the screen").** Armed by a TINY SELF-CONTAINED SCRIPT at the top of `<body>` that adds `body.intro` on phone + home + motion — it must run before the FIRST PAINT, because the main script sits at the end of body and the browser can paint before it (a full page for one frame, then hidden, is exactly the flash this exists to prevent). **The arm script may reference nothing from the main script (the TDZ law).** A deep link never arms — that page has no logo to draw. The driver at the script's tail poses the logo off its real seat (a FLIP to ~78vw centre on an inline transform; `.intro-pose` gates the pre-pose flash) and walks four classes on timeouts: `.intro-shrink` at 4650 (the pose transitions home over .85s while `.fveil` ghosts from opaque white to the frost, so the page appears BEHIND it), `.intro-tick` + `.intro-bar` at 5650 (the tick's first decode — `startTick` is DEFERRED at boot and called here instead, one starter with two callers called exactly once — and the bar pieces ride in, brand from −70vw, picon from +70vw), `fveil.down()` at 6400, `clearIntro` at 7350, with a 12s hard failsafe. **THE POSE RIDES A 0-TIMEOUT, NOT rAF** — it still runs after `route()` has booted the page, but a frozen frame loop (background-tab load, the Claude pane) must not stall the whole intro. `.fveil` itself is the shared focus organ documented in [mobile.md](mobile.md) — z 6400, a new ladder rung, with the masthead lifted to 6450 so the drawing logo rides above it.

## The spine: hero video + the story figure

`#spinevid` is the ONE `<video>` on the page — `site/media/covercard.mp4` with `site/media/covercard-poster.jpg`, autoplay/muted/loop/playsinline. **It MOVES rather than duplicating:** `placeVideo()` (bound to a `matchMedia("(max-width: 940px)")` change listener and called at boot) reparents the element itself between `#video-wrap` in the spine on desktop and a `.tframe` inside `#vtile`, the mobile tile grid's first tile — one element, two seats, so playback position and decode state survive every crossing. It only rolls while `#home` is actually showing (`route()` pauses it on every non-home branch and plays it on the way back). **Clicking it opens `#vlb`** — the video lightbox, whose cover slot IS this element (see [lightbox.md](lightbox.md)); `closeVlb` hands it back through `placeVideo()` still playing. Under it, `.note` states the honest caption ("all shredding in the above video was performed on originals"). **THE STORY FIGURE** is `.made-note` + `.made-row`: the line "originals, made like this" over a `<figure>` holding `site/media/story-clamps.jpg` (a deck clamped up with every clamp in the shop) beside `.pitch` — "all proceeds to fund the tools / R&D for advanced dorking in v2". **The spine's own Interested?/DM row RETIRED 2026-08-08** on Dylan's cross-out: the lightbox CTA, the drawer's DM and the footer carry the contact story, and a fourth door in the spine was the one nobody used.

## The home bands + the conveyor

`HOME` is the const the whole home page is built from: `COLLECTIONS.map((c, i) => ({ c, i })).filter(x => x.c.boards.length)` — **a collection with no boards is not a band, not a tile and not a tab**, and that one filter is why an emptied collection never leaves a hole anywhere. Three consumers read it: `#topnav` (an `All` tab, one tab per collection, then `#nav-lisst`), `#cols` (the desktop bands), and `#tilegrid` (the mobile tiles). A band is `<section class="band" data-col=i style="--acc:accOf(c)">` holding a `.bandhead` (an `h2` with `role="link"`, printing `SHORT[c.id] || c.title` inside `.hl`, plus `.bandsub` printing `SUB[c.id] || c.tagline`) over `<div class="belt" data-col=i>`. The h2 is a real door — click or Enter/Space sets `location.hash`.

**THE CONVEYOR is the shop's whole verb, and it is a RECYCLER, not a scroller.** `rails[]` holds one rail per `.belt`; `buildBelt(rail)` repeats the collection's boards enough times to cover `max(innerWidth + spacing*4, 3200)` at `spacing = cardW + 44` — **the +44 is room for the 1.14× centre zoom**, so a card at centre never crowds its neighbours. One `frame(now)` rAF loop poses every rail: `rail.offset` drifts at `BASE_V` (−26 px/s), the pointer steers it (past one `spacing` from centre the target ramps to ±(60 + 340t), eased in at `dt*4`), and each card's pose is derived from its distance `d` from centre through `smooth(0.62, 2.6, d)` — **scale `1.14 − .36·fall`, opacity `(1 − .62·fall)` further multiplied by an EDGE term so cards dissolve into the ground rather than clipping at the belt's border** (`.belt::before/::after` are the matching ground-coloured gradient masks, ground-swapped under `body.light`), and `z-index` = `1000 − d*100` so the centre card paints over its flanks. **`--shx` is the shadow's own drift** (±14px, `(center−cx)/spacing × 9`): the hard shadow enters offset toward the incoming edge, sits directly under the card at shelf centre, and exits leading the other way — the card and its slab are one object per the SHADOW LAW, and this is that law in motion. **THREE THINGS THE LOOP MUST NOT STOMP, all bitten:** deck holes (a card carrying `.indeck` is written `visibility: hidden` every frame — an inline `"visible"` would beat the class), a `rail.frozen` rail (the inspected card's own row holds its gap and skips its pose pass, which is what preserves the lifted card's inline-hidden hole), and an open `#vlb` (the loop early-returns entirely — the VIDEO lightbox pauses everything, the card lightbox deliberately does not). Rails idle out of view via an `IntersectionObserver` at `rootMargin: "120px"`. **Drag is pointer-tracked** (`.belt.dragging`, offset written directly, `rail.moved` accumulated) and **a click only inspects if `rail.moved <= 6`** — a drag must never open a lightbox. `buildBelt` re-runs on a debounced (180ms) resize whenever the CSS `--cardW` breakpoint has flipped or the window outgrew the loop, because a frozen rail spacing under a wider card crowds the frames into each other; the same debounce re-runs `setTbh()` (`--tbh`, the topbar height the lightbox sheet and every ceremony seat measure from), `lockCardScale()` and `applyDeckHoles()` (rebuilt belts come back with every card visible). **Belts stay INSPECT-ONLY** — moving targets plus a recycler is the wrong place for a second verb, and touch never sees them at all (`display: none` under the dock gate, so an open lb costs nothing on the phone).

## The mobile home: the tile grid

`#tilegrid` renders one `.tile` per `HOME` entry as a door-shaped card, not a photo: `.tcard` = white card, inner frame, `.tcount` chip at the corner, the collection's cutout (`CUT(c.boards[0])`), `.tnm` the bold name over it, and `.tsub` printing "N boards" behind a `.tdot` — **the card reads as a DOOR to a section rather than a board**, which is the whole reason it isn't just the first card of the collection. `#vtile` is tile one and holds the hero video on this side. ## The collection page, shop-all, and the foot

**NO COLLECTION GETS ITS OWN PAGE.** `renderColPage(c, i)` builds `order = [i].concat(every other index)` — the selection leads and then the whole set continues below it, one scroll through everything on every screen size. `renderShopAll()` is the same thing without the reorder and with no masthead of its own: the page opens straight onto the first collection, because the header lives WITH its section. Both end with `<section class="colpage-inner shopall-foot">` + `colnavHtml()`, both call `bindScards()` then `landWants(colpage)` (the phone's want-chip words).

`colSectionHtml(c, i, afterH2)` is the shared body: `.secbody` wrapping `h2` (`titleHtml`) + `.desc` (`BARLINE[c.id] || SUB[c.id]` — the SHORT line since 2026-08-08's "shorten our subtext"; the full `.description` retired with the section bar) + `.shelfgrid` of `.scard`s, or `.restock` when a collection is empty. **`.secbody` exists for the scroll relay** — it is the sticky pin whose `<i class="runway">` sibling is the paused-scroll budget the relay consumes; inert on the phone and asleep site-wide for v1 behind `RELAY_ON` (see header-bar.md). **A COMMENT SITS OUTSIDE THAT `return` ON PURPOSE, and the note is worth more than it looks:** a multi-line comment between `return` and its value is a LineTerminator, ASI closes the statement, and the function silently returns `undefined` — it cost a render of six empty sections with zero console errors.

**`lockCardScale()` IS THE PROPORTION LOCK:** shelf-grid cards render at `CARD_REF_W` (232px, the j-card spec) and are `transform: scale`d down into their slot, with the `.scard`'s height clamped to the scaled card — so a narrow-grid card is a photographic REDUCTION of the desktop card, never the desktop chrome squeezed onto a smaller footprint. Desktop slots are 232 so it is a no-op there: **one continuous law, no breakpoint.** It must run after visibility settles — a hidden grid measures 0 — which is why `route()` calls it after flipping `hidden` and the resize debounce calls it again.

**`route()` is the router and it does more than route.** In order: `sendUnlock()` and `body.dealing` off (a route mid-ceremony must never leave the page sealed or frozen), both lightboxes closed (`closeLb(true)` / `closeVlb(true)`), `body.on-picks` toggled, the branch render (`shop-all` / `my-lisst` / a collection / home), `lockCardScale()`, `applyDeckHoles()`, `stackDealt = false` + `benched.clear()` + `renderPicks()` (the deck repopulates on the way OUT — see [picks.md](picks.md)), the landing, then the ceremony and the relay reset. **THE LANDING IS `behavior: "instant"` AND IT IS LOAD-BEARING (2026-08-15):** `html` carries `scroll-behavior: smooth`, so the old plain `scrollTo(0,0)` ANIMATED — a route fired from a scrolled page painted the NEW page at the OLD offset and glided ~420ms to 0 under the reader (measured 515 → 515 → 484 → 208 → … → 0). **That single line was two separate complaints** — Dylan's "strange collections viewing" AND the drop menu's page-jump — because every hash change shares this function. The same lesson `unlockScroll` paid for on 08-08. It is also what lets the relay's door-state law hold (scroll is genuinely at 0, so every boundary re-arms and the bar's word is earned by scrolling, never granted by the click) and what makes the picks ceremony's first beat paint at the top of the page with the grid already at final geometry.

**THE FOOT GOES HOME (2026-08-15, the launch-day trim — Dylan: "remove the old buttons from the bottom of the collections scroll... change that to the same logo as our top left page header, and give it '· home ·' underneath it").** `colnavHtml()` is now one door: `.colnav > a.colnav-home` = the bar's own logo (56px, circular) over `·&#8195;HOME&#8195;·`. The loop icon (`LOOP_SVG`) and the per-collection chips are retired — **the drop is the nav now, so the scroll's end states ONE door.** The mark is ground-swapped at render (`logo-circle.png` on light, `logo-line-white.png` on dark) and LIVE-SYNCED by `setGround`, which rewrites every `.colnav-home img` src from the bar logo's — one source, so the foot can never disagree with the header. The back-link retired in the same trim. **Note the second producer:** `renderLisst`'s desktop branch also emits `colnavHtml()`; the phone's picks page passes `""` instead (the page ends on its send ceremony).
