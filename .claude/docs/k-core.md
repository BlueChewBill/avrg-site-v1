**His notes, verbatim:** *"D is the move. move the draw in up so its 2/3 up the page instead of dead center. as soon as the logo starts to makes it move towards its landing lets break our site fade / load in into chunks - originals - then shaped - then routed- also logo lands and video comes in from left of screen. then clamp photo behind it."*

**The ride:** the mark draws BIG in the **upper third** of a white screen → it starts home, and on that same beat the page builds itself in **collection chunks** (a chunk = one band, its head and its shelf together, in his order originals → shaped → routed) → the mark seats, the veil rides down, the **video slides in from off-screen left** → the **clamp print lands behind the video**.

**The beat map** (two derived numbers, no third — `DRAW_HOME` is the shared const the phone intro also reads, `LAND_SHRINK` mirrors the .85s brand flight in CSS, and `SEAT` is their sum): `0` pose at ⅓ height · `2375` shrink starts + chunk 1 · `2615` chunk 2 · `2855` chunk 3 · `3225` SEAT — tick, bar, veil down, video from left · `3850` clamp print + spine tail · `4450` clear. The last chunk lands ~3355, just past the veil drop, so the build happens **under the frost** and the focus line passes pieces already arriving — the thing that made D read the way it did.

**CHUNKS ARE KEYED BY COLLECTION IDENTITY, never document order** — the driver stamps `data-land-chunk` on each band by looking its id up in `COLLECTIONS`, so a reshuffle of the data cannot silently reorder his ceremony, and a band that never got a stamp is swept up by the tail beat. The old `nth-of-type(2..6)` ladder is gone with it.

**Gates:** desktop 941+, motion allowed, bare home route, and **only `d`** — `?land=a|b|c` now read exactly like no param. An explicit `?land=d` **always plays** (once-per-session is a bake-time policy call about a shipped ceremony, deliberately kept out of temporary code).

**The fence, four sites in `index.html`** (all marked `DEV SWITCH — the landing, remove when it bakes`): the CSS block at the foot of the sheet (inside `@media (min-width: 941px)`, every rule scoped `body.land` / `body.landed`); the arm script at the top of `<body>` beside THE INTRO ARM; the `.land` term on the `startTick` boot gate; the driver at the script's tail beside the intro's.

**THE CLAMP PRINT IS THE ONE NEW OBJECT, and it is a design guess awaiting his react.** A `::before` on `.video-wrap` (no markup, lifts out with the block), sized by `aspect-ratio: 9/14` off the video rather than inset off its seat — `.video-wrap` is taller than the video because it carries the caption, and an inset box reached down over that line. It peeks **up-and-right** (14px / 26px) instead of the site's usual down-right, because down is the note and right is the empty column gutter. **Its classes outlive the ride** (`body.landed` / `.landed-in` are NOT in `clearLand`'s list): this is the one beat that *leaves* something rather than revealing markup that was always there, and a print that lands then blinks out at the clear is not what "then clamp photo behind it" asks for. **The cancel machinery survives the rework:** every beat rides a flushed `timers[]`, with a `hashchange` listener and a live `matchMedia` re-read + `change` listener, plus a self-contained 12s belt in the arm (the only failsafe that survives a dead main script). Cancel lands the video and the print **at rest, never half-slid** — the classes come off together and the CSS resting state IS the settled page.

**Bitten during the build, worth keeping:** the video's slide SNAPPED while its fade played, because the shared hold selector is *more specific* than the video's own rule and **a state's `transition` REPLACES the base list rather than merging** (the `.fveil` law in gotchas, hit from the specificity side). The fix is one organ, one list — the video is excluded from the shared hold and carries its own.
