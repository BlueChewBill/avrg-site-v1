## Home page logo + THE SITE THESIS + progressive disclosure

- Home page logo — **LANDED 2026-08-16: the single-stroke draw-in (his SVG, inlined; see k-core.md).** The remaining half of thread 1 is the landing AROUND it (tagline/cards choreography, replay policy). The original note stands for tone: over-the-top is on-brand (AVRG = "average" / "a very real goober" — the joke is a maximal site for a humble name). The site's thesis: the cards and coherent animation are what keep a visitor past 5 seconds; the polish is the setup and "it's just a fingerboard, man" is the punchline — the intended discovery ladder is angry cart → a "MINE!" decode → actually reading the taglines, at which point *you know that he knows*. **PROGRESSIVE DISCLOSURE IS DELIBERATE (Dylan, 2026-07-27): "im kind of into the idea of parts of the site not making themselves obvious. its fine that you can navigate the whole thing and never realize you can chunk a card in or out of the drawer deck manually."** Undiscovered depth is a feature here, not a usability debt — the same thesis as the ladder above, applied to interaction. **THE RULE THAT KEEPS IT SAFE: every hidden path has a VISIBLE TWIN.** Drag-to-deck is a shortcut for what the +List chip and the lightbox button already do in plain sight, which is exactly why hiding it costs nothing. Apply this as a test to anything new: hidden *delight* is on-brand, hidden *necessity* is a bug. It bears directly on the undecided contact flow — contact is the one path that must never need discovering.
